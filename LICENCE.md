## 🧩 About

**Deadlock Crosshair** is a comprehensive overlay for Deadlock featuring custom crosshairs, recoil compensation, hitmarkers, and dynamic crosshair adjustments.

Based on popular tools like **Custom Crosshair**, **Crosshair X**, and **HudSight**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Crosshair Customization
- Custom Crosshair — choose from dozens of styles, colors, and sizes
- Dynamic Crosshair — changes based on movement and shooting
- Hitmarker — visual and audio hit confirmation
- Crosshair Outline — improve visibility on any background

### 🔫 Recoil & Accuracy
- Recoil Compensation — reduces weapon recoil pattern
- Spread Control — tightens bullet spread
- No Recoil — completely removes recoil (optional)

### 🖥️ Overlay & HUD
- Custom HUD — reposition health, ammo, and abilities
- FPS Counter — real-time performance monitoring
- Stream-Proof Mode — hide overlay from OBS and other capture software
